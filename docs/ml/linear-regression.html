Linear regression

ML Fit a line to 20 synthetic points

Insight

Minimize squared vertical distances (residuals). Closed form w = (XᵀX)⁻¹ Xᵀy jumps to the unique minimum of a convex loss bowl.

1. Data and fitted line

20 points from y = 2.5x + 3 plus noise. Least squares recovers the line in one shot.

Green = data · blue = fitted line

2. Residuals

Red segments are vertical errors. Squaring them penalizes large misses more.

3. Loss surface

MSE vs slope and intercept — a convex bowl. Green dot is the optimum.
